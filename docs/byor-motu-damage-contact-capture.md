# Native source damage contact capture

The generic private producer derives a damage request from the actual retained HitBlock damage ticket and collision decoder. It captures physical attacker, target, source attack index, frame, handle, attack identity, time and canvas generation. Source force is copied from the compiled source attack table. A repeated capture preserves the same request and continuation cursor; a new request resets them. The collision decoder is reset before each query, and sticky faults are rejected.

The 412-case native join executes capture twice per contact (824 calls), followed by the actual opponent, damage drop, effects, facing and common health services. All 388 admitted contacts reach health exactly once; 24 malformed contacts reject. Full actor and retained state observations remain checked by the joined fixture. Bank 0 is unchanged.

World global inputs and authored actor configuration remain installed by the fixture. The full player/common callback, score, death and action continuations and production world producer are not bound. This is a contact producer proof, not MOTU gameplay certification. No color reduction or PCM copying occurred. See the [receipt](receipts/byor-motu-damage-contact-capture.json).
